| `trackingtest3_2` | 2026-11-01 | Repeat of test 3 (schema v2, white bars on constant black, landscape 874×402 pt, rightward 90 pt/s). iPhone 16 Pro (`iPhone17,1`), iOS 26.6.2, session `5E5CDC71`. 599 samples, 60 Hz, 100 % tracked, no blinks, no gaps, no issues. Slow phase is opposite to the stimulus *if* the unverified eye-angle sign is right. Plots: `trackingtest3_2.png`, `trackingtest3_2_okn.png`. |
